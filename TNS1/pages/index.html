Our Commitment

Fishing Is in Our Blood.
So Is Responsibility.

We've fished Atlantic Canadian waters for over three decades. We've watched the ocean change — and adapted. That's why we work exclusively with MSC-certified suppliers, practice minimum-bycatch methods, and never sell what the sea can't replace.

Every product ships with a full chain-of-custody record: vessel name, harvest date, and water origin. Because transparency isn't a feature — it's the standard.

Why It Matters

Built on Sustainable Principles

Ocean Health First

We only fish species at sustainable population levels and rotate zones to allow natural recovery.

Zero-Waste Packaging

All shipping boxes are curbside-recyclable. Ice packs are reusable or compostable — nothing to landfill.

Community-Owned

We partner with small family-run fishing operations in Nova Scotia and PEI — keeping profits local.

Full Traceability

Every product ships with a QR code linking to catch data, vessel name, and harvest date.
